## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The hero/nav/footer bands are genuinely authored for FACT — violet floral photography, countdown, Fraunces/Jost, ink-900 footer. Past the hero, the roster body (`.team__groups` → `.team__grid` → `.team__member`) is a generic "grouped directory" template: circle-initial avatar, bold name, uppercase role, no cards. The same radial-gradient orb is reused verbatim for Past FACTs and the nav badge — a system-wide utility device, not something authored for a team roster specifically. Nothing in the composition (grouping, avatar shape, label hierarchy) touches Mahiwagahan's "mystery/magic/wonder" theme beyond ambient palette and a near-invisible 0.24-opacity orbit motif. Swap the palette and this reads as a generic SaaS "our team" page.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI `detect.mjs --json team.html` — clean, 0 findings. The live-render overlay (a separate rule set from computed styles/DOM, injected via `live-server.mjs`) reported 38 anti-patterns: 4 `low-contrast`, 32 `ai-color-palette` (one per `h2.groupheading` and `div.team__avatar` — repetitive, same finding on every instance), 1 `cream-palette` on `body`.

**False positives:** All 4 `low-contrast` flags (hero `h1`, `.hero__eventline`, hero `p`, `.pill--ghost`) are almost certainly false positives — the tool computed contrast against the page's cream body-background color rather than the dark purple hero image/gradient the text actually renders over. Screenshot evidence (zoomed) shows crisp white-on-dark-purple text, not 1.0:1. The 32 `ai-color-palette` + 1 `cream-palette` flags are stylistic/heuristic "this looks AI-generated" signals, not correctness bugs — they inflate the "38 anti-patterns" headline without representing 38 distinct issues.

**What the detector missed that the LLM review caught:** the actual accessibility defect on this page — `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--cream-100) }` (styles.css) applied globally, so every keyboard-focused link on the parchment day band (all 11 jump-nav links, both crosslink links) gets an outline color identical to its own background (`rgb(255,255,221)` on `rgb(255,255,221)`, confirmed via computed style, not the mechanical scanner). This is a real WCAG 2.4.7 failure the automated tooling did not surface at all — Assessment A found it, Assessment B's overlay did not.

**Convergent gap:** both assessments independently hit the same tooling limit — `resize_window` reported success but the tab's rendered width stayed at 1280px in both agents' sessions — so neither could visually confirm the site's own documented "Open item — not independently verified" mobile/900px-breakpoint behavior. Treat mobile rendering as still unverified, not as confirmed-fine.

## Overall Impression

The night/day/night band system and hairline discipline are executed with real consistency — this isn't a sloppy build. But the roster body itself is the generic part of an otherwise-specific page, and the one accessibility defect that actually exists (invisible keyboard focus) is exactly the kind of thing a purely visual pass — human or automated — would miss, because it only shows up when you tab through the page rather than look at it.

## What's Working

- **No-card, hairline-only system reads as an index, not a corporate grid** — whitespace and Fraunces headings alone carry structure, matching the "almanac" concept better than boxed cards would, verified identical across Workshops/Past FACTs.
- **Roster reflects FACT's actual 11-committee structure**, not a generic Leadership/Marketing/Ops abstraction — jump-nav maps 1:1 to real group names, building credibility for a prospective delegate.
- **Accessibility plumbing beyond the visual layer is more considered than it looks**: `.team__grid`/`.team__member` promoted to `<ul>/<li>` for assistive-tech list announcement; disabled pills use `aria-disabled` + `.sr-only` reasons instead of native `disabled`.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Invisible keyboard-focus outline on the entire day band.**
Why it matters: `outline-color: var(--cream-100)` in the global `:focus-visible` rule exactly matches the parchment background color every jump-nav and crosslink link sits on. Every keyboard-only visitor tabbing through team.html gets zero visible indication of focus position — a real WCAG 2.4.7 failure, not a cosmetic nit, and it wasn't caught by the mechanical detector.
Fix: give day-band focus states their own token (`outline-color: var(--accent-on-light)` or `var(--ink-900)`) scoped to `main`, distinct from the night-band default that the cream outline was tuned for.
Suggested command: /impeccable audit

**[P1] Colliding avatar initials undermine the one differentiating cue the avatars offer.**
Why it matters: 5 people share "J" (Josh Barlan, Jacob Daza, Josrich Viernes, Joshua Jimenez, Joshua Fajardo), several share "M"/"L"/"R". The avatar visually promises differentiation it doesn't deliver, forcing the user back to reading names anyway — pure extraneous cognitive load.
Fix: drop the placeholder avatar until real photos land (name/role typography alone already carries the roster with less visual noise), or switch to two-letter initials in the interim.
Suggested command: /impeccable distill

**[P1] Jump-nav has no "you are here" state and no way back to the top.**
Why it matters: the index's sole job is wayfinding across 11 groups, but there's no scroll-spy highlighting of the current section and no back-to-top control once scrolled past the nav.
Fix: lightweight IntersectionObserver to underline the active `.team__jumplink` (main.js already handles similar interactivity elsewhere), plus a simple back-to-top link near the footer.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P2] Single-member groups (Hospitality, Treasurer) create visual imbalance.**
Why it matters: a lone 96px avatar in an otherwise-wide flex row reads as unfinished next to the 2-3-person rows around it.
Fix: cap `.team__grid` width for single-member groups, or pair the row with a short one-line group description so it doesn't read as empty.
Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P2] Undefined Filipino-language committee names ("Palengke") for a stated non-exclusive audience.**
Why it matters: PRODUCT.md states facilitators/delegates "come from varied backgrounds" and the site should read as welcoming beyond one ethnicity, but nothing glosses what Palengke or Team FACT actually do.
Fix: a one-line description per group heading — also closes the "what does this group actually do" gap for all 11 groups, not just this one.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

## Persona Red Flags

Riley would reasonably conclude keyboard nav is broken even though tab order itself is fine.

## Minor Observations

- DESIGN.md documents `.team__avatar` as "68px"; shipped CSS is 96px desktop / 76px mobile — doc/code drift worth reconciling (`/impeccable document`).
- Group ordering ("Directors first, then alphabetical") has no visible explanation of the sort logic — low-cost, fine to leave.
- The corner orbit motif (0.24 opacity) is nearly invisible against parchment and doesn't meaningfully differentiate Team from Workshops (butterfly) or Past FACTs (moon) on a quick scan.
- Long names ("Kasandra Medrano," "Josrich Viernes") at a 170px flex-basis column may wrap unevenly at intermediate widths — not verified live due to the resize-tooling gap.
