## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Not a swappable shell. The trailer opens on a custom "FACT presents" title card in the site's own violet gradient, sparkle, and butterfly vocabulary — real marketing collateral staying on-brand, not stock footage standing in. The Fraunces variable-axis type, the hand-tuned five-stop hero gradient, and the sparkle motif threading from the Theme moment through the CTA seal icon are all deeply specific to this product.

**A genuine disagreement surfaced between the two independent assessments and was resolved directly, not left standing:** Assessment A reported the trailer modal's focus trap holding correctly (8 Tab presses stayed inside, reaching only YouTube's own player chrome). Assessment B reported the opposite — Tab presses from the close button allegedly reaching "About page carousel" navigation buttons elsewhere on the page, calling the trap broken. **Independently re-tested by hand after the disagreement**: index.html has zero `.photocarousel__nav` elements anywhere on the page (that component only exists on about.html) — Assessment B's own evidence describes DOM elements that cannot exist on the page it was supposed to be testing, indicating its test session had cross-contaminated tab/page state. A clean re-test (real click to open, real Tab key presses, `document.activeElement` checked after each) confirmed: focus moves close-button → briefly reports as `<body>` for one tick (a known browser artifact of focus entering a cross-origin iframe) → lands on and stays on the `<iframe>` element itself for 8+ consecutive Tab presses, `insideModal` true throughout, never once landing on `<header>` or `<main>` content. **Assessment A's finding stands; Assessment B's contradicting claim on this specific point does not.**
